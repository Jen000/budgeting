Understand your money without handing it to anyone.

Your files never leave your device. Nothing is saved. Refreshing this page erases everything.

Everything runs inside this tab. There is no server, no account and no tracking. To keep your results, use Export → Save as PDF.

1. Upload your statements

Add bank statements, credit-card statements and loan statements. You can add several files, including ones whose dates overlap; duplicates are removed automatically.

Most accurate: CSV or OFX/QFX exports from your bank's website. Text-based PDF statements also work, but PDF reading is imperfect, so you will review every row before it is used. Scanned or photographed documents are not supported.

Paychecks

Enter your pay to get an accurate income figure. Deposits found in your statements are used if you skip this. You can also upload a text-based PDF paystub to pre-fill this form; you always confirm the numbers.

2. Review & categorize

Check categories and choose what counts. Transfers between your own accounts and credit-card payments are excluded from spending, so nothing is counted twice.
